# About the guide

I wrote this guide because I’ve spoken with a lot of trans people here in Denmark who are stuck on CKI’s waiting lists, and not everyone is aware that there are other options, or they’re overwhelmed by how complicated and intimidating the process can seem. The goal is to gather the best information and organize it to make it easy to understand.

Since testosterone is classified as a performance-enhancing drug by the Danish Medicines Agency, it is illegal to possess it without a prescription. So even though I’d really like to, this guide cannot cover DIY masculinizing hormone therapy.

Everything in this guide is based on my own experiences and on conversations I’ve had with other trans people. I have no medical training, and the guide may contain errors. If you find something that’s incorrect or if you have information you’d like to add, please send an email to [dktransguide@pm.me](mailto:dktransguide@pm.me).
