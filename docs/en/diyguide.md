# DIY Guide

The DIY guide will cover the different resources and information that you will need if you want to use DIY hormone therapy, but it also covers some things that are good to know if you are in treatment at a private clinic.

## Resources

There are many different websites and forums with information about DIY hormone therapy. One of the largest resources is [r/TransDIY](https://www.reddit.com/r/TransDIY/). Their [wiki](https://www.reddit.com/r/transDIY/wiki/index/) and [FAQ](https://www.reddits://www.reddit.com/r/r/transDIY/wiki/index/) have really great information about DIY hormone therapy and are a great place to start. If reddit says you need to verify your identity to see it, I would recommend using a VPN or using a reddit frontend like [Redlib](https://http.com/redlib-ordlib-instances/blob/main/instances.md).

Another really good website is [diyhrt.info](https://diyhrt.info/). They have a lot of information about DIY hormone therapy, and I would recommend that you read their entire transfem/transmac guide if you are interested in DIY hormone therapy or just hormone therapy in general.

[Trans Harm Reduction](https://transharmreduction.org/) is a really good website for various things, but one of the most important things they do is testing the hormones from different sellers so we can be sure that the hormones are safe and effective.

[TransfemScience](https://transfemscience.org/) is another great page with lots of information about hormone therapy. I would highly recommend reading their article [An Introduction to Hormone Therapy for Transfeminine People](https://transfemscience.org/articles/transfem-intro/) as it has lots of important information which is good to know if you want to start DIY hormone therapy.

## Sellers

## Blood tests

If you want hormone therapy, blood tests are always very important. We usually take blood tests before we start hormone therapy, and then again 3 months after we have started. Then we also keep an eye on our levels every 6-12th months, or every time there are changes to our dosage. 

Generally, before starting hormone therapy, we will take the following blood tests: 

- Full blood count (white blood cells, red blood cells, haemoglobin, platelets, haematocrit, mean corpuscular volume )
- Liver Function tests (ALT, AST, ALP, bilirubin)
- Fasting glucose 
- Urea and electrolytes/ Kidney panel (Creatinine, eGFR, Urea, Potassium, Sodium) 
- Lipids /cholesterol panel (total cholesterol, triglycerides, HDL, LDL)
- Prolactin (PRL)
- Testosterone and Estradiol (Total Testosterone and E2) 

When we are on HRT and taking our follow-up blood tests, we usually take these blood tests:

- Liver Function tests (ALT, AST, ALP, bilirubin)
- Urea and electrolytes/ Kidney panel (Creatinine, eGFR, Urea, Potassium, Sodium) 
- Lipids /cholesterol panel (total cholesterol, triglycerides, HDL, LDL)
- Prolactin (PRL)
- Testosterone and Estradiol (Total Testosterone and E2) 

If you are on [cyproterone acetate](https://min.medicin.dk/Medicine/Contents/318) (often Androcur), it is very important that you keep an eye on your prolactin levels in particular. Cyproterone acetate has a small chance of giving [prolactinoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prolactinoma), which is a benign tumor in the pituitary gland. It's not a common side effect of cyproterone acetate, but if your prolactin levels are very high, it's important to talk to your doctor. 

When you need to have blood tests, you may be confused about where you need to have them, as your own doctor will most likely not take them. The best solution in my experience is to have the blood tests done at [Dansk Sundhedsteam](https://ds-vt.dk/). You can order blood samples at them, then they will add them to the system so that you can book an appointment for blood tests at a hospital in your region. It’s a great option because you don’t have to go to their clinic but can instead have them taken at a hospital near you. After you have taken them, the blood samples are sent to their lab. Once they've done their job, they'll send you the results digitally. 

It can be a bit expensive, but in my experience it's the cheapest option you have. If you are in treatment at Imago, however, you can mention it to them, and they can then deduct VAT from the price so you can save some money.
