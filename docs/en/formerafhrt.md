# Forms of HRT

## Estradiol
There are many forms of estradiol, each with its own advantages and disadvantages, as well as varying degrees of availability.

### Pills
Pills are the most popular and well-known option, and they are easy to obtain if you're doing DIY. The downside is that they can be relatively expensive. Estradiol pills can be taken orally or via sublingual/buccal administration. Most doctors recommend taking the pills orally twice a day, once in the morning and once in the evening. This generally results in more stable levels than sublingual/buccal administration.

Sublingual administration involves placing the pill under the tongue and allowing it to dissolve, while buccal administration involves placing the pill between the gum and the inside of the cheek to dissolve. These two methods of administration essentially yield the same result.

Sublingual/buccal administration leads to higher peak concentrations of estradiol in the blood, which then drop rapidly. Therefore, it is recommended that sublingual administration be performed three times daily at 8-hour intervals.

Some people choose sublingual/buccal administration because the medication bypasses the liver. It's believed that this reduces the risk of blood clots and may provide better efficacy. However, there is only anecdotal evidence for this, and research is lacking.

### Transdermal
Transdermal treatment (estradiol applied to the skin) is available in forms such as gels, patches, and sprays.

Patches are typically changed once or twice a week and are designed to deliver a fixed dose of estradiol daily. They are typically placed on the lower abdomen or the upper buttocks. Note that there has been a global shortage of patches for some years, making it very difficult and expensive to obtain them through unofficial channels.

Estrogen gel is typically available in sachets or pump bottles that dispense a fixed amount with each use. Estradiol gel is formulated with an alcohol base to enhance absorption. It is usually applied to the skin once daily.

Spray products are typically sold by homebrewers and contain estradiol in an alcohol-based solution applied to the skin, usually via a spray bottle. Sprays can be applied to the inner thighs, forearms, or potentially the scrotum for better absorption, as the skin is thinner in those areas.

### Injections
Estradiol injections are the most common method of administration for DIY hormone therapy. Injections are usually the most effective and affordable option.

Estradiol is typically produced in an esterified form so that it remains in the body longer. If regular (non-esterified) estradiol were injected, it would break down very quickly, therefore, all injectable estradiol is esterified. After injection, the body slowly converts the ester back into regular estradiol, allowing the substance to remain in the body for a longer period.

The most common estradiol esters are valerate, cypionate, enanthate, and undecylate. Each has a different half-life, indicating how long the substance remains in the body. 

## Anti-androgens
There are various anti-androgens, but they all primarily come as tablets to be taken orally.

The most well-known anti-androgens are:

- Spironolactone (Spiro)
- Cyproterone acetate (Cyproterone, Cypro)

Spironolactone is a weak anti-androgen, but it is also very safe. Common side effects include increased thirst, salt cravings, and frequent urination. Spironolactone is **not recommended** as a first-choice anti-androgen due to its weak anti-androgenic effect.

Cyproterone is a highly effective anti-androgen when combined with estrogen. High doses should be avoided due to a more problematic risk profile. It is important to stay below a certain dosage, especially since cyproterone is typically sold in doses 4–8 times higher than those recommended for transfeminine individuals.

If you are taking [cyproterone acetate](https://min.medicin.dk/Medicin/Indholdsstoffer/318), it is very important to monitor your prolactin levels closely. Cyproterone acetate carries a small risk of causing a [prolactinoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prolactinoma), a benign tumor of the pituitary gland. While this is not a common side effect of cyproterone acetate, it is important to consult your doctor if your prolactin levels are very high.

## Progesterone
Progesterone is one of the two primary female sex hormones. It is not essential for a successful transition, but many find that it aids breast development, has positive effects on mood, and can increase libido. It is recommended to start progesterone only after having taken estrogen for 6–12 months.

Progesterone is primarily available as tablets, though some homebrewers also sell transdermal progesterone. The tablets are taken either orally or rectally. Rectal administration is believed to offer better absorption and fewer side effects. When taken rectally, rinsing the tablet in lukewarm water can help, as it makes it easier for the tablet to dissolve. If that isn't enough, you can also try pricking a small hole in the tablet with a needle. It is recommended to take the pill before going to bed, as lying down afterward helps, and because there is less chance of needing to use the toilet during the night.

Progesterone is usually taken once a day. If taken orally, it is recommended to do so in the evening, as many people find it makes them very tired.

## Testosterone
There are several different forms available, each with its own advantages and disadvantages, as well as varying levels of difficulty in obtaining them.

### Injections
Injections are the most popular and accessible method for DIY hormone therapy, as they allow for the easiest and most consistent dosing.

Testosterone is usually produced in an esterified form so that it remains in the body longer. If pure (non-esterified) testosterone were injected, it would break down very quickly, therefore, all injectable testosterone is esterified. After injection, the body gradually converts the ester back into regular testosterone, allowing the testosterone to remain in the body for a longer period.

Common esters include cypionate, enanthate, and undecanoate. Different esters have different half-lives (the time the substance remains in the body). At the same dosage, esters with shorter half-lives produce higher peak concentrations but for a shorter duration. Esters with longer half-lives result in lower peaks and act over a longer period, while also allowing for less frequent injections.

In short, the term "half-life" describes how long the drug remains active in the body. 

### Gel
Testosterone gel is generally harder to find at retailers, but it is usually subject to less scrutiny by customs when ordered from abroad. Testosterone gel can be just as effective as injectable testosterone. It typically needs to be applied to the skin daily. The product is available in either sachets or pump bottles.

If you obtain testosterone with a prescription from a private clinic, it will most likely be gel.
